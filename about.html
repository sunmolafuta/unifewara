About the University of Ifewara

Our history, our vision, our mission and the people who lead the University of Ifewara — a university of technology in Osun State, Nigeria.

Our Story

A University Born to Build

The University of Ifewara was established by statute in 2009 to address the shortage of technologists and technical professionals in the South-West of Nigeria. What began with two departments and 180 students has grown into five schools, twenty departments and more than twelve thousand students from fourteen countries.

The University is located in Ifewara, a town of rolling terrain, cascading waterfalls and productive farmland, about thirty kilometres from Osogbo. The surroundings are not incidental to our mission: the teaching farm, the dam and the forest reserves around campus are part of the laboratory.

Today the University is a full university of technology, offering undergraduate, postgraduate, part-time, weekend and distance programmes across science, agriculture, information technology, environmental studies and engineering.

Our Purpose

Vision, Mission and Core Values

Mission

University Officers

Leadership and Administrative Officers

The University is led by the Vice-Chancellor, assisted by two Deputy Vice-Chancellors, the Registrar, the Bursar and a team of directors. These officers report to the Governing Council and the Senate.

Governance

Governing Bodies at a Glance

A three-tier governance model separates policy from academic authority and daily administration. Full composition details are on the structure page.

Governing Council

Sets policy, approves the budget, appoints officers and authorises major capital projects. Meets twice a year.

Senate

The academic authority. Approves programmes, regulations, examinations, results and the award of degrees. Meets monthly.

Executive Committee

Handles urgent operational matters between Senate meetings and reports to Senate for ratification. Meets fortnightly.
