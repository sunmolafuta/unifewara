Welcome to Ifewara

A University of Technology Built for Africa's Future

Founded in 2009 in the heart of Osun State, the University of Ifewara was created to close the gap between classroom theory and industrial practice. Our programmes are co-designed with employers, our laboratories are shared with industry, and our graduates leave with the ability to build things that work.

  • Five schools of science, agriculture, information technology, environmental studies and engineering.
  • Twenty departments, each with its own laboratories, workshops and industry partners.
  • Undergraduate, postgraduate, short-course, part-time and distance learning routes.
  • Compulsory SIWES and industrial attachment for all undergraduate programmes.
  • Technology incubation, entrepreneurship training and start-up support.

Admission Process

Six Routes Into the University

Whatever your background, there is a clear route into the University of Ifewara. Every route ends with a documented, verifiable process.

UTME / JAMB

Five credits and a competitive UTME score, followed by the compulsory post-UTME screening exercise.

Direct Entry

OND, HND, NND, NCE, A-Level or City & Guilds candidates apply directly online.

Pre-Degree Programme

A one-semester bridging programme in Mathematics, English and Computer Studies.

Postgraduate

M.Sc., M.Eng. and Ph.D. applications with aptitude tests and panel oral interviews.

International Students

A dedicated portal, credential evaluation and acceptance letter for visa processing.

Part-time & Distance

Weekend and online delivery through the Directorate of Distance Education.

Administrative Structure

Governed by Council, Accountable to Senate

The University operates a three-tier structure: the Governing Council sets policy, the Senate governs academics, and the University Executive Committee handles day-to-day operations. Ten directorates and central units support the five schools and twenty departments.

Governing Council

Supreme governing body. Approves policy, budget and major capital projects.

University Senate

Academic authority. Approves programmes, results and the award of degrees.

Schools & Departments

Five deans and twenty heads of department deliver the academic programme.

Central Units

Registry, Bursary, Library, ICT, Health, Estates, HR and Security units.

One login, everything academic

Your studies, fees and results in a single portal

Enrolled students sign in with the matriculation number and PIN issued on admission. No downloads, no queues at the faculty office — everything is done online and stays available to you for the whole session.

Course registration

Pick your courses for the term, watch your unit load against the minimum and maximum, and confirm once. The timetable and lecturer details are generated immediately.

Results and transcript

Continuous assessment and examination marks for every level, with your GPA, classification and a downloadable transcript that carries your matriculation number.

Fees and receipts

Your full bill itemised, settled line by line or as a single payment. A receipt reference is issued for every transaction and stays on your record.

Hostel and library

Check your hall, room and key card, log maintenance faults, and see the books you have on loan with due dates and any lending penalty.

Already have a reference number?

Track a new application through screening, departmental review and admission without creating an account. You will get a reference as soon as you submit the form.
