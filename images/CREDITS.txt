IMAGE CREDITS
==============

IMPORTANT
---------
Every photograph in this folder is a Creative Commons (CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA /
public domain) picture downloaded from Wikimedia Commons. Several were taken at
universities inside Osun State and neighbouring states so the site looks right
while you build it.

They are STAND-INS. They are NOT photographs of the University of Ifewara.
Replace every file in this folder with your own photographs of the real campus
before publishing. If you replace the images, delete this file.

The attribution below is required by the CC BY and CC BY-SA licences.
