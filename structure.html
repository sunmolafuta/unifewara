Administrative Structures

How the University of Ifewara is governed: the Council, the Senate, the Executive Committee, ten directorates, central units, five schools and twenty departments.

Organisation

Four Tiers of Authority

Authority flows from the Governing Council down to the University, the schools and the departments. Each tier has clearly defined duties and reporting lines.

Tier 1

Governing Bodies

Governing Council, Board of Trustees and the University Committees. They set policy, approve the budget and authorise major decisions.

Tier 2

University Leadership

Vice-Chancellor, Deputy Vice-Chancellors, Registrar, Bursar, Provost, Librarian and the ten directorates that run University-wide functions.

Tier 3

Schools

Five schools, each led by a dean, with an assistant dean for administration and a faculty board of studies handling academic matters.

Tier 4

Departments & Units

Twenty departments led by heads of department, with laboratories, studios, workshops and departmental non-teaching staff attached to each.

Staff Establishment

Academic and Non-Teaching Staff Categories

Academic staff

  • Professors — the highest rank, appointed on scholarly achievement
  • Readers — senior research specialists
  • Senior Lecturers — experienced teachers and researchers
  • Lecturer I and II — full-time teaching staff
  • Graduate Assistants and Interns — teaching and research support

Non-teaching staff

  • Senior Management — registrar, bursar, librarians, directors
  • Professional staff — engineers, architects, accountants, lawyers, librarians
  • Administrative staff — officers, assistants, registry and bursary clerks
  • Technical staff — technologists, technicians, laboratory attendants
  • Support staff — drivers, store keepers, cleaners and artisans
