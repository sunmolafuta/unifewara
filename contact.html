Find Us

University Location

Ifewara, Osun State, Nigeria — approximately 30 km from Osogbo along the Osogbo–Ibadan road.

From Osogbo

About 30 kilometres, roughly forty-five minutes by road. Campus shuttle buses leave the Osogbo junction at 6:30am, 12:30pm and 4:00pm on weekdays.

From Ibadan

About 60 kilometres. Take a bus to Osogbo and connect to the University shuttle, or use the intercity staff bus that leaves campus at 6:00am daily.

From Lagos

Approximately 180 kilometres. Use the Lagos–Ibadan expressway, continue on the Ibadan–Ifewara road, or book a University-arranged airport pickup at Murtala Muhammed airport.
