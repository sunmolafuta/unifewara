Life on Campus

A safe, green and well-equipped campus in Ifewara, Osun State: libraries, laboratories, the teaching farm, halls of residence, a health centre, sports and a thriving student union.

Facilities

Teaching, Research and Living Facilities

Every facility below is available to students, staff and approved visitors. Priority during examinations and registration periods is given to students.

Student Experience

Beyond the Lecture Hall

The University supports a wide range of clubs, societies and programmes that develop leadership, discipline and enterprise.

Students' Union

An elected union representing every student, with a working executive and a physical secretariat in the union building.

Robotics Club

Open to students of Computing and Engineering; competes regionally and builds autonomous machines.

Environmental Science Club

Tree planting, water-quality monitoring and community conservation campaigns around the campus.

Open Source Society

Runs code clinics, hackathons and contributions to open-source projects for students across all schools.

Debate Society

Weekly debate sessions and preparation for national and international speaking competitions.

Multimedia and Journalism

Runs the student radio station, the campus newspaper and a film and photography unit.

Service Learning

Community outreach in neighbouring villages: teaching, water testing and health education.

Entrepreneurship Hub

Mentoring, incubation space and a small-grant fund for student start-ups.

Student Support

Services That Support Your Degree

Counselling

Academic, career and personal counselling by professional counsellors, free for all registered students.

Library services

Study halls, lending, e-resources, remote access and research support for postgraduate students.

Health

A 24-hour emergency service with a pharmacy, medical laboratory and referral to teaching hospitals.

Disability support

Ramp access, accessible lecture rooms, braille and audio materials, plus a disability liaison officer.

Bursary and welfare

Book loans, the student food subsidy and emergency support for students in distress.

Career services

CV clinics, mock interviews, employer fairs and graduate tracking after graduation.

Visit Us

Planning a Visit

Prospective applicants, parents and international delegations are welcome to visit. Please arrange your visit in advance so that we can host you properly.

Getting here

Ifewara is about thirty kilometres from Osogbo. From Lagos, take the Lagos–Ibadan expressway to Ibadan and continue on the Ifewara road. Campus shuttle services run from the Ibadan bus terminal.

By air

The nearest airports are Osogbo (about 35 km), Ibadan (about 60 km) and Lagos Murtala Muhammed International. The University arranges airport pickup for visiting delegations.

Book a visit

Send at least three working days' notice to the Directorate of International and Linkages stating your date, party size and area of interest.
