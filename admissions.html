Admission Process

Everything a prospective applicant or visitor needs to know about joining the University of Ifewara: routes, requirements, documents, fees, timetable and what happens after you are admitted.

The General Admission Process

Whatever your route, the University follows the same eight-stage process from application to registration.

Choose a programme and check the cut-off

Use the programme catalogue to confirm the award, duration and departmental cut-off for the session.

Register an applicant account

Create an account on the University admission portal with a valid email address and phone number.

Complete the online application form

Fill in personal details, academic history, programme of choice and upload scanned certificates.

Pay the application fee

Pay through the portal, by bank transfer or at any designated bank, then upload your receipt.

Submit and print your slip

Review every detail, submit the application and download your application slip or examination number.

Sit the screening or aptitude test

Post-UTME candidates sit the screening exercise; postgraduate candidates sit the written aptitude test and oral interview.

Accept the offer and pay tuition

Accept your offer within the stated deadline on the portal and pay first-year tuition and other charges.

Matriculate and register

Report for medical screening, original document verification, course registration and orientation.

Entry Requirements

Prepare Your Files

Documents Required

Scan every document as a clear PDF or JPEG, no larger than 2 MB each. Files that cannot be read will delay your application.

Undergraduates

  • JAMB result slip and printout
  • O-Level / SSCE / NECO result (or NND, NCE, A-Level)
  • Birth certificate or passport page
  • School leaving certificate
  • Passport photograph (recent)
  • State of origin or nationality proof

Postgraduates

  • Bachelor's degree certificate (or provisional)
  • Academic transcripts
  • NYSC discharge or exemption letter
  • O-Level certificate
  • Research proposal for Ph.D. applicants
  • Passport photograph and identification

International applicants also upload a valid passport, an evaluation report from the relevant authority and proof of financial capacity.

Life After Admission

What Happens Once You Are Admitted

1. Registration

Course registration online, then confirmation of your registered courses at the departmental level.

2. Lectures Begin

Teaching starts the second week of September. Each lecturer issues a course outline in the first week.

3. Practical Work

Laboratory, workshop and field sessions are compulsory. Attendance below seventy per cent disqualifies you from the course.

4. SIWES

Students in the fourth year undertake a minimum of twenty-four weeks of industrial training.

5. Examinations

End-of-semester examinations, then a resit session for failed courses before the final year.

6. Project

A final-year project or design supervised by an academic member of staff of your department.

7. SIWES Reporting

Your industrial training logbook and report are assessed by the host organisation and your department.

8. Graduation

Projects are defended, results are approved by Senate and degrees are awarded at the annual convocation.

Steps 1, 2 and 6 are all done in the student portal

As soon as your admission is confirmed you receive a matriculation number and a PIN. Sign in to register your courses, view your timetable, check your results and transcript, and settle your fees without queuing at the bursary. You can also see your hostel allocation and library loans from the same login.
