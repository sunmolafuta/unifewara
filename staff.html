Career Structure

Academic Ranks and Administrative Cadres

Academic Cadre

  • Professor — highest academic rank; appointed on sustained scholarly contribution
  • Reader — senior research specialist with national recognition
  • Senior Lecturer — experienced teacher and active researcher
  • Lecturer I — full teacher with independent teaching responsibility
  • Lecturer II — entry level for freshly qualified graduates
  • Graduate Assistant — teaching and research support during postgraduate study

Non-Teaching Cadre

  • Senior Management — Registrar, Bursar, Provost, Librarians, Directors
  • Professional — Engineers, Architects, Accountants, Auditors, Librarians, Counselors
  • Administrative — Senior Administrative Officer down to Clerical Officer
  • Technical — Technologist, Technician, Laboratory Attendant, Craftsperson
  • Support — Driver, Store Keeper, Cleaner, Gardener

Vacancies and Career Opportunities

The University recruits academic and non-teaching staff regularly. Vacancies are advertised in two national newspapers, on the University notice board and on this website.

  • Academic positions: Professor, Reader, Senior Lecturer, Lecturer I and II.
  • Professional positions: engineers, architects, accountants, librarians, ICT specialists.
  • Administrative and technical positions in every directorate and central unit.
  • Graduate assistant opportunities for candidates pursuing a postgraduate degree.
