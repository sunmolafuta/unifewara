Admission Requirements

How Programmes Are Structured

Undergraduate, Postgraduate and Short Courses

Level 1

Undergraduate (100–400 Levels)

Five-year programmes of eight semesters, comprising General Education courses, departmental core courses, electives, a compulsory SIWES attachment in the fourth year and a final-year project or design.

  • Admission by UTME, Direct Entry or Pre-Degree
  • Award: B.Sc., B.Eng., B.Tech., B.Agric.
  • Minimum CGPA for graduation: 2.00
Level 2

Postgraduate (500–600 Levels)

M.Sc., M.Eng. and M.Tech. taught programmes run for four semesters, with coursework, examinations and a research project. Ph.D. candidates enrol in a residency programme of six to ten semesters.

  • Admission with a second-class upper degree
  • Aptitude test and oral interview required
  • Award: M.Sc., M.Eng. or Ph.D.
Level 3

Short Courses & Certificates

Professional diplomas and certificates of three to six months for graduates, teachers, artisans, public servants and SME operators. Delivered in the evenings and on weekends.

  • No JAMB requirement
  • Recognised by SON and relevant professional bodies
  • Certificate awarded on completion
