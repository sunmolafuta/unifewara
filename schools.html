How We Operate

Teaching, Research and Community Service

Teaching

Course delivery supported by laboratories, workshops, field visits, clinical placements and the teaching farm. Continuous improvement is monitored by the Directorate of Quality Assurance.

Research

Research clusters in solar energy, cybersecurity, food security, water resources, materials and machine learning, funded through TETFUND and industry partners.

Community Service

Extension services to farmers, water quality testing for communities, free ICT literacy clinics, health outreach and school mentoring programmes.
